# Design direction: Digital-First Financial Platform

**Branch:** `design/digital-first`

## Design objective

Present RapidLink as a modern, efficient financial-services platform prepared
for expanded digital banking and future self-service capabilities.

## Visual principles

- compact information architecture and fast task access;
- official blue used confidently with orange as an action signal;
- crisp panels, controlled gradients and abstract financial graphics;
- mobile-first navigation and persistent access to common tools;
- prominent digital channels, service notices and account/product discovery;
- lightweight motion with strong performance and accessibility limits.

## Prototype emphasis

The homepage should foreground digital channels, current information and common
customer tasks. Product pages should support quick comparison and decisive next
steps. News, service notices, mobile services and future ATM or exchange-rate
features should fit naturally into the system without being falsely presented
as currently available.

## Guardrail

Change presentation only. Do not alter content records, calculations, rates,
authentication, Supabase policies, routes or regulatory wording.
