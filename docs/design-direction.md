# Design direction: Corporate Trust

**Branch:** `design/corporate-trust`

## Design objective

Present RapidLink as an established, dependable and well-governed financial
institution. The interface should feel formal and calm without appearing dated.

## Visual principles

- structured grids and disciplined alignment;
- navy-led surfaces with official blue and orange used selectively;
- restrained corner radii, shadows and animation;
- clear hierarchy, generous white space and prominent trust information;
- strong product comparison, rates, contact and institutional sections; and
- professional Ghanaian customer and staff photography.

## Prototype emphasis

The homepage should lead with institutional confidence, current services and
clear product entry points. Product pages should prioritize requirements,
benefits, rates and next actions. The Institutional Profile, branch information
and customer-support routes should be especially prominent.

## Implemented architecture

- formal utility header and full-width editorial mega menus;
- split-image advisory carousel with numbered controls;
- customer-intent navigator, product directory and structured news grid;
- permanent institutional sitemap with a separate adviser call-to-action; and
- mobile navigation and footer disclosures adapted to the same hierarchy.

## Guardrail

Change presentation only. Do not alter content records, calculations, rates,
authentication, Supabase policies, routes or regulatory wording.
