# Design direction: Human-Centred Banking

**Branch:** `design/human-centred`

## Design objective

Present RapidLink as an approachable financial partner that helps Ghanaian
individuals, families and businesses achieve practical goals.

## Visual principles

- customer-led photography and clear real-life financial goals;
- warm neutral backgrounds supported by the official blue and orange;
- friendly typography, rounded but professional cards and inviting spacing;
- product discovery organised around customer needs and outcomes;
- visible support, community impact and customer guidance; and
- purposeful, subtle movement that respects reduced-motion settings.

## Prototype emphasis

The homepage should help visitors choose goals such as saving, growing a
business or accessing responsible credit. Product pages should use plain
language, progressive disclosure and obvious help routes. Community impact,
customer support and branch access should feel integrated rather than secondary.

## Implemented architecture

- floating capsule navigation with compact card popovers;
- image-led story carousel with a warm, conversational content card;
- life-goal product journeys and a customer-focused product story;
- branch cards and upward-opening footer link drawers; and
- rounded, touch-friendly mobile interactions throughout.

## Guardrail

Change presentation only. Do not alter content records, calculations, rates,
authentication, Supabase policies, routes or regulatory wording.
